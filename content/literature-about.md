Natasha S. is a poet, translator, journalist and editor. Born in Russia, she has lived in Iceland since 2012. She holds a BA in journalism from Moscow and a BA in Icelandic as a second language, with Swedish, from the University of Iceland. Her first collection of poetry, <em>Máltaka á stríðstímum</em> (2022), received the Tómas Guðmundsson Literary Prize and was nominated for the Maístjarnan Award. Her second, <em>Mara kemur í heimsókn</em> (2025), won the Maístjarnan Award in 2026. She edited and co-authored two anthologies of writing by authors of foreign origin, and in 2023 received the Icelandic Language Committee's recognition for extending Icelandic as a literary language to those who did not grow up with it.
