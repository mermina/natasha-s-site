About

Natasha S.

Natasha S. is a poet, translator, journalist and editor. Born in Russia, she has lived in Iceland since 2012. She holds a BA in journalism from Moscow and a BA in Icelandic as a second language, with Swedish, from the University of Iceland. Her first collection of poetry, Máltaka á stríðstímum (2022), received the Tómas Guðmundsson Literary Prize and was nominated for the Maístjarnan Award. Her second, Mara kemur í heimsókn (2025), won the Maístjarnan Award in 2026. She edited and co-authored two anthologies of writing by authors of foreign origin, and in 2023 received the Icelandic Language Committee's recognition for extending Icelandic as a literary language to those who did not grow up with it. She photographs writers, readings and people at home.
